KARNATAKA STATE POLICE
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP) MANUAL
Crime Investigation & Case Handling Guidelines
Issued by: State Crime Records Bureau (SCRB)
Version: 2026.1

===============================================================
SECTION 1: FIR REGISTRATION PROCEDURE
===============================================================

1.1 Filing a First Information Report (FIR)
- Every complaint disclosing a cognizable offence must be registered as an
  FIR under Section 154 of the CrPC without delay.
- The Station House Officer (SHO) is responsible for ensuring the FIR is
  registered within 24 hours of receiving the complaint.
- A copy of the FIR must be provided to the complainant free of cost.
- Zero FIR: If the offence occurred outside the jurisdiction of the police
  station, a Zero FIR must be registered and later transferred to the
  appropriate jurisdiction.

1.2 Classification of Offences
- Cognizable offences (murder, robbery, rape, kidnapping) require immediate
  FIR registration and investigation without magistrate approval.
- Non-cognizable offences (minor assault, defamation) require magistrate
  permission before investigation can begin.

1.3 Severity Scoring Guidelines
- Severity scores (0-10) are assigned based on: nature of offence, use of
  weapons, extent of injury/loss, vulnerability of victim, and repeat
  offender status.
- Scores 8-10: Heinous crimes (murder, rape, terrorism) - Highest priority.
- Scores 5-7: Serious crimes (robbery, kidnapping, grievous hurt) - High
  priority, senior officer supervision required.
- Scores 1-4: Property crimes, minor offences - Standard investigation
  timelines apply.

===============================================================
SECTION 2: INVESTIGATION TIMELINES
===============================================================

2.1 Standard Timelines
- Simple cases: Investigation to be completed within 60 days.
- Cases involving offences punishable with death/life imprisonment:
  Investigation to be completed within 90 days (as per CrPC 167).
- Cybercrime cases: Preliminary digital evidence collection within 48 hours
  to prevent data loss.
- Cases against women and children (POCSO): Investigation must be
  completed within 2 months of recording the FIR.

2.2 Case Status Categories
- Pending Investigation: Active case under investigation.
- Under Trial: Chargesheet filed, case before court.
- Convicted: Court has delivered a guilty verdict.
- Acquitted: Court has delivered a not-guilty verdict.
- Closed (Untraced): Investigation concluded without identifying accused.

===============================================================
SECTION 3: ARREST AND CUSTODY PROCEDURES
===============================================================

3.1 Arrest Guidelines
- Arrest memo must be prepared for every arrest, countersigned by a family
  member or respectable person of the locality.
- Arrested person must be informed of the grounds of arrest immediately.
- Medical examination is mandatory at the time of arrest and release.
- Arrested person must be produced before the magistrate within 24 hours,
  excluding travel time.

3.2 Wanted / Absconding Accused
- If an accused evades arrest despite due process, they are classified as
  "Wanted" or "Absconding" in the case records.
- Proclaimed offender status may be sought from the court under CrPC 82
  after repeated failure to appear.
- Look Out Circulars (LOC) should be issued for accused likely to flee the
  jurisdiction or country.

===============================================================
SECTION 4: CRIME HOTSPOT & PATROLLING GUIDELINES
===============================================================

4.1 Hotspot Identification
- Stations with case counts exceeding the district average by 20% or more
  should be flagged as hotspots for resource reallocation.
- Average severity score above 6.0 combined with high case volume warrants
  additional patrol deployment and CCTV surveillance installation.

4.2 Preventive Patrolling
- High-severity hotspots require night patrol frequency of at least once
  every 2 hours.
- Community policing initiatives should be prioritized in districts with
  recurring domestic violence or kidnapping patterns.

===============================================================
SECTION 5: PROPERTY & EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT
===============================================================

5.1 Stolen Property Recovery
- All recovered property must be logged with photographs, description, and
  estimated value within 24 hours of recovery.
- Property recovery rate is a key performance indicator (KPI) for station
  performance evaluation.

5.2 Chain of Custody
- Physical evidence must maintain an unbroken chain of custody from
  collection to court presentation.
- Digital evidence (CCTV, mobile data) must be hashed and stored in
  write-once media to prevent tampering allegations.

===============================================================
SECTION 6: CRIMINAL NETWORK & GANG MONITORING
===============================================================

6.1 Gang Classification
- Repeat offenders with 3 or more FIRs and known associations are flagged
  for criminal network analysis.
- Co-accused appearing together in 2 or more FIRs are considered part of
  the same network cluster for intelligence purposes.

6.2 Habitual Offender Monitoring
- Habitual offenders (History Sheeters) must be periodically verified by
  the local police station as per the History Sheet maintenance guidelines.
- Districts with rising gang-related cybercrime or vehicle theft trends
  should coordinate with the Cyber Crime Cell and CID for joint operations.

===============================================================
SECTION 7: CASE CLEARANCE RATE (CCR) TARGETS
===============================================================

7.1 Definition
- Case Clearance Rate = (Cases Chargesheeted + Cases Closed) / Total Cases
  Registered, expressed as a percentage.

7.2 Targets
- District target CCR: 60% or above annually.
- Districts falling below 40% CCR require a review by the Superintendent
  of Police (SP) and a corrective action plan.
